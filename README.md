# The Meridian Wire — Sunday 22 November 2026

One page, one day: the whole of Sunday 22 November 2026 as a single
1024×1024 wire-service front page — 36 stories, every one of them an event
of that day, every number on the page bound to a story on the page.

The date is the masthead, set at 93% of the page width, because the date
is the news. Directly under it, the italic subdeck is the day's three
leads in one line. The numbers strip runs six red serif figures — 720/9 ·
$120bn · 2 · M4.1 · −18 · 11 — each owned by a story below it, and the
region tally segments all 36 stories (Middle East 6 · Europe 8 · Oceania
7 · Asia 5 · North America 10) so the count can be checked at a glance.
A seven-slot UTC timeline runs from the 01:42 UTC interception
confirmation to the 16:30 UTC kickoff at Anfield.

Three pictorial leads lead the page — the Gulf overnight complex, the
COP31 final text, and India's 720/9 at Wellington — each with a red fact
bar on its scene, a second-number chip, a region slug and a certainty
tag (REPORTED / CLAIM / FORECAST, defined in the footer legend). Twelve
pictured minis follow the same discipline: every card pictured, every
card tagged, every card sourced. The dark map panel carries numbered
pins for the pictured stories, the also-covered list completes the 36
in two columns, and the DATE CHECK box names what was left out and why:
misdated stories, not-yet events, and the two Gulf claims that remain
unconfirmed.

## Hand-in

`main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` into the directory named by
`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). The image is the deliverable;
nothing is re-rendered at grade time.
