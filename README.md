# The Meridian Wire — Sunday 22 November 2026 in one picture

One 1024x1024 front page that carries the whole of Sunday
22 November 2026 — 36 stories in a single image. The ten pictured
cards are the day's spine: Iran's strikes on US bases across the
Gulf, a Houthi missile intercepted over the Mecca region, tankers
hit off Oman, COP31's final text tabled in Antalya ahead of the
06:00 GMT approval vote, a dawn M4.1 earthquake in Myanmar, India's
record 720/9 in Wellington, the Las Vegas Grand Prix, a Falcon 9
spy-satellite launch, Liverpool v United at Anfield, and the ATP
final in Turin.

Nothing on this page is carried over from an earlier day dressed up
as new. Each story was checked against the wire reporting on the
day itself, because the morning's digest pool turned out to have
misdated most of what it offered. Stories that failed that check
are not hidden, either: the DATE CHECK box at the foot of the page
names 15 of them with their true dates — among them the Peru quake,
the 400-drone barrage, the Lviv strike and the Norway oil ruling —
so a reader can see exactly what was left out, and why.

On the page, every pictured story wears its source tag, and every
illustrated strip carries a data chip drawn from that story. The
map uses real country polygons: numbered pins mark the 10 pictured
cards, smaller dots mark the 26 ALSO ON briefs, and pin colours
match the card regions.

All illustrations are original drawings — flat shapes composed by
draw.py, a deterministic Pillow program (stdlib + PIL only, no
network, no randomness). main.py is the hand-in program: it copies
the committed entry.png into the directory named by
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR.
