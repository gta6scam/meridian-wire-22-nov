# The Meridian Wire — Sunday 22 November 2026 in one picture

A single 1024x1024 front page summing up Sunday 22 November 2026: 36 stories
— Iran's strikes on US bases across the Gulf, a Houthi missile intercepted
over the Mecca region, tankers hit off Oman, COP31's final text tabled in
Antalya with the approval vote at 06:00 GMT, a dawn M4.1 quake in Myanmar,
India's record 720/9 in Wellington, the Las Vegas Grand Prix, a Falcon 9
spy-satellite launch, Liverpool v United at Anfield and the Turin final.
Every story on the page was re-verified against the wires on the day: the
morning digest pool had misdated most of its items, so nothing old is
dressed as Sunday's news.

Every pictured story carries its source tag on the page, and each illustrated
strip carries the story's own data chip. The map is drawn from real country
polygons, with numbered pins for the 10 pictured cards and dots for the 26
ALSO ON briefs; a DATE CHECK box names 15 stale stories deliberately left
out — with their true dates, including the Peru quake, the 400-drone
barrage, the Lviv strike and the Norway oil ruling that are NOT today's
news.

All illustrations are original drawings — flat shapes composed by draw.py,
a deterministic Pillow program (stdlib + PIL only, no network, no randomness).
main.py is the hand-in program: it copies the committed entry.png into the
directory named by MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR.
